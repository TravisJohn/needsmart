Where this started

It began as my own side project, with one simple idea.

Before the business was interested, NeedSmart was a tool for my own household. The extra layers on this page, like cohorts, models and privacy design, are the proposal for taking it to members. None of that is built yet. The core is still the simple idea.

The original idea · my indie project

A need-based alert. Every week it scanned the specials at Coles and Woolworths, matched them to what my household was predicted to need, and turned that into a smart grocery plan.

The problem

Members do the same mental maths every week, alone.

Most of a household's shop is a repeat of the last one. Yet every week they rebuild the list from memory, scan thousands of specials, and can't tell whether a "saving" actually saved them anything.

Remembering

"Do we need coffee yet?"

Lists are rebuilt from memory. Things get forgotten, triggering a top-up trip, or double-bought and wasted.

Searching

"Which of these specials matter to me?"

The catalogue is huge and mostly irrelevant to any one household. Finding the few deals that match what they actually buy is work.

Trusting

"Did I actually save $40?"

"Was" prices measure against the shelf, not against what this household normally pays. Members are rightly sceptical.

NeedSmart turns each member's receipt history into a short, checked, personal list and tells the truth about savings.

Knows what you need, and when

Predicts at the need level ("yoghurt"), not the exact barcode, from each household's own purchase rhythm.

Finds the best-value way to buy it

Matches due needs to this week's specials, multi-buys, pack sizes and own-brand swaps the household is likely to accept.

Shows savings you can believe

Every figure is recomputed by a number-checker and measured against what this household usually pays, then confirmed from the next receipt.

Answers questions in plain language

"Why is milk on my list?" An assistant answers from the member's own data, with the maths done by tools, not guessed.

Testing the core premise

We only remind people when we're confident we're right.

A wrong reminder costs trust. So before we build, we'd run four simple checks on our own eReceipts. Each one protects that trust, and together they tell us exactly how many members the feature can reach.

The result: about 4 in 10 members get confident "you'll need this by Thursday" reminders. Everyone else still gets a useful list and deals, and moves up as their history grows.

Filter 3 · Will they switch brands?

Swaps that learn: offer when they don't mind, stop when they stick

For consistent shoppers, we measure whether the brand matters to them within a need. NeedSmart offers a cheaper alternative only where it doesn't, and watches what they do next.

How we measure indifference
Switch rateHow often the brand changes between buys of the same need
Special-drivenDo switches follow another brand's special?
Return rateAfter a switch, do they go back to their usual brand?
What NeedSmart does with the score (0 to 1)
0.6 and aboveIndifferent: offer the cheapest acceptable option
0.3 to 0.6Unsure: test one swap, then watch the next buys
Below 0.3Loyal: never swap; flag their brand's specials instead

Two declined swaps and NeedSmart stops offering for that need. It only tries again if the member switches on their own. Scores are illustrative.

    Why the personal list runs on the phone

    Same data we already hold. A safer place to use it.

    We have the full purchase history, so we could build every list centrally. We choose not to. The history teaches the model; each member's list is worked out on their own phone.

    Central · de-identifiedFull purchase historyPremise study, need-groups, model training
    On the phone · personalThe member's eReceiptsTheir own list, predictions and savings

    No new profile to hold

    Predictions like "this household will need nappies on Thursday" never reach our systems. We can't misuse, leak or be asked to hand over what we don't have.

    Less to protect

    No new central dataset means no new breach risk and an easier privacy review. To "what are we storing?", the answer is nothing new.

    Near-zero compute cost

    Re-forecasting hundreds of needs for millions of members runs on their phones, not our cloud. Only shared packs and models are built centrally, once.

    Trust members can feel

    "Your list is worked out on your phone, from your own receipts." Own-brand swaps then read as advice, not a sales push.

    Simple consent and control

    It uses receipts members already see. If they turn NeedSmart off, the list is gone, with nothing to clean up on our side.

    Fast and offline in store

    The list works with no signal in the aisle, and updates instantly when a member ticks or swaps.

    "Wouldn't central be simpler?" Yes, for us. On the phone is safer for members and cheaper at scale. Training stays central, so model quality doesn't suffer.

    Nothing new to collect

    Runs on eReceipts members already have. No extra scanning or data entry.

    Whole packs only

    Shared data is downloaded in full and matched on the phone.

    Every number checked

    A figure that fails the checker is withheld, never shown.

    Basket, not person

    Health insight describes the basket. No diet or medical advice.

    Why us, why now

    We hold the three ingredients an outside app can't get.

    A third-party app would need members to scan paper receipts and would guess at products. We already have clean receipts, product data and offers. The value to the business below is framed as hypotheses to prove in a pilot, not promises.

    Our advantage

    Full history to learn fromYears of linked, itemised purchases already in our systems. De-identified, they power the premise study, the need-groups and model training. No new data collection.
    eReceipts for the personal list, on the phoneEach member's list is worked out on their own phone from eReceipts they already get. We hold no new profile, pay almost nothing per member, and every prediction points to receipts they can open.
    Real product dataThe product hierarchy replaces fuzzy name-matching, so need-groups and Health Star Ratings come straight from the source.
    Our own offersSpecials, multi-buys and member prices are first-party data, current to the day.
    Scale for benchmarksReal anonymised cohort tables make "households like yours" meaningful.
